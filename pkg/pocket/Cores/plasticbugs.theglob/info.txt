The Glob
Epos Corporation, 1983

Eat the snacks, dodge the frogs, ride
the elevators. The Epos Tristar 8000
board: a Z80 at 2.75 MHz, a bitmap
through a colour PROM, an AY-3-8912.
Checked against MAME frame by frame.

* D-pad       move
* A / Y       energy (button 1)
* B / X       button 2
* Select      coin
* Start       1 player start
* R shoulder  2 players start

Menu: lives, difficulty, bonus life,
coinage and demo sounds are the
board's DIP switches. Audio Filter
(Light by default) softens the ticks
of the jump sound; Cabinet Reverb
adds a room. Service Switch opens the
game's own diagnostics.

ROMs are not included. Build the image
from your own MAME theglob romset
(the parent suprglob's colour PROM is
needed too) with mra_build.py and
theglob.mra, and copy it to
Assets/theglob/common/theglob.rom
