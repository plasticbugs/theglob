The Glob
Epos Corporation, 1983

Epos Tristar 8000 board: a Z80 at
2.75 MHz, a 4-bit bitmap through a
colour PROM, and an AY-3-8912.

ROMs are not included. Build the image
from your own MAME theglob romset with
mra_build.py and theglob.mra, and copy
it to Assets/theglob/common/theglob.rom

* D-pad       move
* A / Y       energy (button 1)
* B / X       button 2
* Select      coin
* Start       1 player start
* R shoulder  2 players start
