The Glob / Super Glob
Epos Corporation, 1983

Eat the snacks, dodge the frogs, ride
the elevators. Both games run on the
Epos Tristar 8000 board: a Z80, a
bitmap through a colour PROM and an
AY-3-8912. Checked against MAME.

* D-pad       move
* A / Y       energy (button 1)
* B / X       button 2
* Select      coin
* Start       1 player start
* R shoulder  2 players start

Menu: the board's DIP switches, Audio
Filter (Light by default) for the
jump sound's ticks, Cabinet Reverb,
and the game's own diagnostics.

ROMs are not included. Build them
from your own MAME romsets with
mra_build.py and theglob.mra or
suprglob.mra, and copy theglob.rom
and/or suprglob.rom to
Assets/theglob/common/
